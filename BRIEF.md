# Project brief: SmartStudy

## The competition
- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Middle school (grades 6–8)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: I cant miss the resignation deadline and the submission deadline(decemeber 23) and I have to have a clear and edited draft

## The problem
- People in school struggle with memorizing a big test, like science and biology, chemistry etc, this is because there is just so much things people can do it all at once
- Impact area: School and learning
- Why it matters (impact): It can make people get better grades, study better, and develop good studying habits. Overall students need better ways to study, remember information, and prepare for tests because traditional studying methods aren't always effective
- Evidence it’s real: "The students mostly used expansive strategies, however, due to some problems (e.g., low prior knowledge, misunderstanding, or inattentive reading), they used limiting strategies (e.g., deducing from the task structure or guessing answers), and failed the tasks. These results offer a solid foundation for subsequent steps toward improving classroom practice, which stresses the need to focus on problem-solving and strategy development more during (chemistry) lessons." - ACS Publications,(https://pubs.acs.org/jceda8/article-abstract/98/6/1831/389604/Students-Procedure-When-Solving-Problem-Tasks?redirectedFrom=fulltext)
- What people told me: Person said " I don't really like using new strategies to study. I mostly use flashcards; it also stresses me out more that we have an entire TEXTBOOK to study in 1 week."

## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by making a customized study guide, which works better than working with a fixed study guide because people might think it doesn't really work for them or in their way, also it can help by putting useful infortmaition instead of all of it at once, using only the important ones.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Quizlet, but at some limitations, because to study more, you have to pay, but while it does use AI, it does not have customized studying, which my projects does have.
- Safety plan: Situation 2: Someone asks for something harmful.
Problem
A student asks the AI how to do something dangerous with chemicals.
Your solution
The AI refuses to provide dangerous instructions and redirects the student to a teacher or trusted adult.

Situation 3: A student feels frustrated.
Problem
A student says, "I'm terrible at science. I'll never understand this."
Your solution
The AI responds kindly, breaks the topic into smaller pieces, and encourages the student to try again without making them feel bad.

## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)
1. The user can pick a test topic and get a customized AI study guide.
2. The user can take a practice quiz made from their study guide.
3. The user can see which quiz answers they missed and why.
4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.
